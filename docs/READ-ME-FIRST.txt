GOOD MIXX  ·  Crate OS by XTRAGOODLABS
Remembers your good mixes and suggests what to play next. Works with djay or Traktor Pro 4 / 3 on a Mac,
with an iPad (or the Mac itself) as the screen.

START
1. Drag GOOD MIXX into your Applications folder, then open it.
   If your Mac says it can't check the app: right-click GOOD MIXX > Open > Open.
   (On newer macOS: System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down > Open Anyway.)
2. The first time, your Mac may ask to install Apple's free developer tools - click Install, then open GOOD MIXX again.
3. GOOD MIXX opens in its own window (quitting GOOD MIXX also stops the iPad screen). The start screen shows the address to type on your iPad
   (iPad and Mac on the same Wi-Fi).
4. Play. Tap GOOD MIX when two songs blend well. Suggestions show your proven mixes first, then BPM + key matches.

DJAY
Just play in djay on this Mac. To read djay's keys, allow GOOD MIXX when asked
(System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility > GOOD MIXX).

TRAKTOR PRO 4 or 3 (one time)
1. Quit Traktor. Double-click "Add Traktor Link" (keep it next to GOOD MIXX). Type your Mac password if asked.
   If it says it couldn't change Traktor's files: System Settings > Privacy & Security > App Management >
   turn on Terminal, quit Terminal (Command+Q), and double-click it again.
2. Open Traktor > Preferences > Controller Manager > Add... > Traktor > Kontrol D2 (you don't need to own one).
3. Open GOOD MIXX and play. Keys show the Traktor way (like 3m) while you're on Traktor.
Undo any time with "Remove Traktor Link". Run "Add Traktor Link" again after Traktor updates.

SET RECORDER (djay on the Mac)
Click SET UP RECORDER at the top of the GOOD MIXX window once, then quit djay and open it again.
From then on GOOD MIXX records your sets whenever djay is open (REC turns it on/off). In STUDIO, sets you
recorded show "● RECORDED" and RECREATE THIS SET rebuilds them as you played them: same start points,
tempo, fader, crossfader, filter and EQ moves. Needs Node.js (brew install node). Only reads djay.

STUDIO (in the GOOD MIXX window on the Mac)
Make a finished mix from your good mixes, or recreate a set you played on this Mac.
- PAST SETS > RECREATE THIS SET, or add songs from MY MIXES / SONGS.
- Pick how songs blend: Beat-matched, Set timing (like you played it) or Crossfade - for the whole mix or per song.
- Set timing (for recreated sets) starts each song from your djay cue 1 - or a later cue if cue 1 is an intro - and
  brings it in when you did, beat-matched. "Start:" under each song picks a different cue. REMOVE QUICK SKIPS drops
  songs you only played for a few seconds.
- ⤢ BIG makes the timeline tall, with bar lines and a volume line. Every song has its own white diamonds: one for how long
  it fades in, one for how long it fades out. Its right edge sets how long it plays under the next song.
  Changing one song never changes the other. Each blend has an FX picker: low-pass out, high-pass out, high-pass in, bass swap.
- Move songs on the timeline: drag a song to bring it in earlier / later (beat-matched blends snap to bars, hold Option for beats).
  Drag a song's left edge to skip into it. ◂ ▸ nudge a bar, ↺ resets, UNDO undoes.
- The timeline shows each song's wave. Press + / - to zoom. ▶ BLEND plays just that blend so you can hear it first.
- BUILD MP3 / BUILD WAV saves the mix and a tracklist to your Music > GOOD MIXX folder.
  SAVE PLAYLIST saves a playlist (.m3u8) + tracklist you can load in your DJ app.
- Only songs saved as files on this Mac go into the audio (streamed songs are left out). FIND lets you point to a file.
- Building audio needs the free tool ffmpeg. If you have Homebrew: open Terminal and type  brew install ffmpeg
  Playlists work without it.

You can switch between djay and Traktor any time - GOOD MIXX follows whichever played last.
To stop: click QUIT in the GOOD MIXX window on the Mac.
Your mixes are kept in: your user Library > Application Support > GOOD MIXX
